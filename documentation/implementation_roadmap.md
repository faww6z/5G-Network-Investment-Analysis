# Conditional implementation roadmap

**Status: proposed, not approved.** Month 0 is the start of a future diligence mandate, not 21 November 2026. The negative base case means the current CAD $50M full build does not enter procurement. Timelines are illustrative and depend on actual site requirements.

| Stage | Indicative window | Work and accountable function | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feasibility | Months 0-3 | Strategy and Finance validate serviceable lines, current coverage and competitors; Commercial tests willingness to pay and distinguishes new from migrating accounts; Engineering defines coverage and capacity requirements | Evidence supports an achievable opening base and price; no unsupported 5G premium; local opportunity and spectrum rights verified |
| Sharing and design | Months 3-5 | Network Engineering compares existing-site reuse, infrastructure sharing, wholesale access and smaller clusters; Procurement obtains budgetary quotes; Finance rebuilds the full cash-flow model | Positive base NPV at 10%, capacity within design, explicit downside and full-lifecycle value review; no automatic credit for hypothetical subsidies |
| Procurement and approvals | Months 5-7 | Procurement obtains executable quotes; Regulatory completes applicable land-use, consultation and technical diligence; Finance fixes contingency and stop criteria | Investment committee approves the revised scope and budget; all required preconstruction clearances complete |
| Pilot deployment | Months 7-10 | Engineering builds a bounded cluster, tests coverage, backhaul, energy resilience and demand conversion; PMO monitors actual spending | Pilot meets agreed coverage/service tests and acquisition economics; revised portfolio NPV remains positive before wider spend |
| Launch and value tracking | Months 10-18 and quarterly thereafter | Commercial launches the approved footprint; Network Operations tracks quality and load; Finance compares actual cash flows with the approved case | Expand only against demonstrated demand, capacity and updated financial returns; correct or stop underperforming clusters |

## Approval conditions

1. **Economics:** a revised design must produce positive NPV after acquisition, tax, working capital, maintenance and realistic timing. Approximately CAD $31.02M is the modeled CAPEX break-even ceiling at base assumptions, not an acceptable price guarantee. A CAD $27.5M quote target provides some room for contingency but requires engineering proof that scope and benefits survive the cost reduction.
2. **Commercial:** validate an achievable opening base. The default model requires about 23,256 opening subscribers and 25,000 annual average subscribers in Year 1. Intent expressions are weaker than price-tested, convertible demand. Lower conversion must enter the model.
3. **Capacity and coverage:** validate capacity against traffic, spectrum, sector design and backhaul. The illustrative 60,000-line proxy is not a substitute. Growth requiring new capacity must include its incremental cost.
4. **Regulatory and delivery:** verify current ISED requirements, rights, site access, consultation, supplier readiness and critical path. Do not treat a consultation proposal as an enacted rule.
5. **Long-term value:** develop a defensible lifecycle forecast or residual estimate. A zero Year 5 residual is deliberately conservative; unsupported continuation value cannot repair a weak case by assertion.

## Illustrative management triggers

These are proposed controls, not company policies. Reforecast if average subscribers or realized ARPU fall below 90% of the approved monthly plan for two consecutive reporting periods; network OPEX exceeds plan by more than 10%; or committed CAPEX plus estimate-to-complete exceeds the approved envelope. Freeze the next deployment tranche until Finance and Engineering explain the impact on NPV. Escalate quality or safety failures immediately under the operator's applicable procedures.
