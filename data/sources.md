## Assumption provenance

`assumptions.csv` labels each numerical input as **Illustrative**, **Control**, or an analyst assumption source. The original brief supplies network CAPEX, horizon, average subscribers, ARPU, growth, OPEX and discount rate. The added 85% incremental share, 27% tax proxy, 10-year life, maintenance, working capital, cancellations, acquisition cost, market size and capacity are illustrative analytical choices.

The fictional regional cluster deliberately has no named municipalities or purported local coverage figures. The next research step would join actual coverage polygons, eligible subscriber lines, competitor offers, anchor-customer evidence and supplier quotes. No such local dataset is available in this case.
